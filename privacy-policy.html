Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Echo ("we", "our", or "us") is a personal voice diary app developed by Dhaval Pandya. This Privacy Policy explains how Echo handles your information.

1. What data Echo collects

Echo is designed to be private by default. All audio recordings, transcriptions, summaries, and memories are stored only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers.

2. Audio recordings

When you record a diary entry, the audio is processed entirely on your device using Whisper, an on-device speech recognition model. Audio files and their transcriptions remain on your device and are never uploaded.

3. Optional cloud AI features

Echo offers an optional feature that lets you connect your own API keys (Claude, OpenAI, or Google Gemini) for enhanced AI summaries. If you choose to enable this:

  • Your diary text is sent to the AI provider you select, using your own API key.
  • This is strictly opt-in — the feature is off by default.
  • We never see, store, or have access to your API keys or the data sent to those providers.
  • Please review the privacy policy of the AI provider you choose.

4. Reminders and notifications

If you enable reminders, Echo uses Android's local notification system. No notification data leaves your device.

5. No analytics or advertising

Echo does not include any advertising SDKs, third-party analytics tools, or tracking frameworks. We do not sell, share, or monetise your data.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone — required to record voice diary entries. Audio is processed on-device.
  • Notifications — optional, used only for reminder alerts you configure.
  • Internet — used only if you opt in to cloud AI features with your own API key.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete individual entries or all data at any time from within the app. Uninstalling Echo removes all app data.

8. Children's privacy

Echo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use of Echo after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
