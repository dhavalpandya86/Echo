## The short version
- Your diary — text, voice recordings, photos, transcripts, and everything Echo derives
  from them (summaries, people, places, tags, "Worlds") — is stored **locally on your
  device**. We do not upload it to our servers. We do not have servers that store it.
- Echo works fully **on-device with no account-provided AI key**. All transcription,
  photo understanding, and analysis then run locally.
- **Cloud AI is optional and off by default.** It runs only if *you* enter your own API
  key for a provider you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic/Claude, or Google Gemini). Then, and
  only then, the relevant memory content is sent to that provider to process your request.
- We do **not** sell your data, show ads, or use your memories for advertising.

## What data Echo handles, and why

**Account (sign-in).** Echo uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in with email or Google.
This processes your email address and a user ID to keep your account. Managed by Google
Firebase under Google's privacy terms.

**Your memories (on device).** Voice recordings, written notes, photos you add, their
transcripts, and derived data (summaries, entities, tags, moods, reminders) are stored in
a local database on your device. They are not transmitted to us.

**Microphone.** Used only while you are recording a memory or dictating. Audio is used to
produce a transcript and is stored locally.

**Camera & Photos.** Used only when you attach a photo to a memory. Photos are copied into
the app's local storage.

**Calendar (read-only).** If you grant calendar access, Echo reads events from the
calendars on your device (including any Google/Samsung accounts you've synced) to display
them alongside your memories. Echo **never creates, edits, or deletes** your calendar
events. This data is read on-device and not sent anywhere.

**Approximate location.** If you grant location access, Echo uses your approximate location
to show local weather on the home screen. Location is used at that moment and is not stored
or shared.

**Notifications.** Used to deliver reminders you create.

## Optional cloud AI (only with your own key)
If you add your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), or Google Gemini in Settings,
Echo will send the relevant content of the memory you're acting on to that provider to:
transcribe audio, describe photos, write summaries, answer your "Remember"/"Reflect"
questions, and extract people/places/topics. Your key is stored on your device and used to
call that provider directly. That provider processes the data under **their** privacy
policy and terms:
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- Google (Gemini API): https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

You can remove the key at any time to return to fully on-device operation.

## Data sharing
We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your memories with third parties,
except the AI provider **you** explicitly enable with your own key (above) and Google
Firebase for authentication.
